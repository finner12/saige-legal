# S(ai)GE Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 6, 2026**

S(ai)GE is an internal business application developed for use by Riley Manufacturing / Riley Sports ("Riley").

## Information S(ai)GE Accesses

S(ai)GE may access business information required to support Riley's internal operations. This may include information obtained through authorized third-party services such as QuickBooks Online, including customer, estimate, order, and related business information.

S(ai)GE may also process information entered or uploaded by authorized Riley personnel, including production information, drawings, shipping information, files, images, and other operational data.

## QuickBooks Online Data

S(ai)GE connects to QuickBooks Online only after an authorized Riley user grants access through Intuit's authorization process.

QuickBooks Online information accessed by S(ai)GE is used solely for Riley's internal business and operational purposes. S(ai)GE does not sell QuickBooks Online data or use it for advertising.

## Data Sharing

Information accessed by S(ai)GE is not sold to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate S(ai)GE or provide its functionality, subject to the applicable services and agreements used by Riley.

## Security

Riley takes reasonable measures to protect information accessed through S(ai)GE and restrict access to authorized users.

Authentication credentials, API secrets, and access tokens are not intended to be publicly disclosed.

## Data Retention and Access

Business information is retained only as required for Riley's operational, legal, accounting, or business requirements and according to the capabilities and policies of the systems used to store that information.

Authorized users may contact Riley regarding access to or questions about information processed through S(ai)GE.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as S(ai)GE and its integrations evolve. Updates will be posted on this page.
